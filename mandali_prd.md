# Mandali App Requirements & PRD

## 1. Project Overview
**Title:** Mandali App Requirements & PRD
**Project ID:** `18213170182407119706`
**Target Audience:** Close-knit groups like Kitty Parties, friend circles, and families.
**Creative North Star:** "The Digital Hearth" – rejecting sterile, cold app designs for a warm, editorial, and "lived-in" feel.

## 3. Design System Strategy: "The Digital Hearth"
- **Primary Color:** #E91E8C (Celebratory Pink)
- **Secondary Color:** #FFB300 (Warm Amber)
- **Neutral Background:** #FDF9F3 (Light Cream)
- **Typography:**
  - **Headlines:** `Noto Serif` (Warm, human, premium).
  - **Body Text:** `Be Vietnam Pro` (Modern, clean, approachable).
- **Visual Principles:**
  - **No-Line Rule:** No thin borders; boundaries defined by color shifts and tonal stacking.
  - **Intentional Asymmetry:** Breaking the "template" look with rhythmic breathing room and slightly overlapping elements.
  - **Physical Layering:** UI treated as stacked surfaces (like paper on a wooden table).
  - **Radiant Actions:** CTAs use subtle linear gradients from primary pink to primary-container.
