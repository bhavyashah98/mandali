Mandali Account Deletion

At Mandali, we value your privacy and your control over your personal data. If you wish to stop using our platform, you can request to delete your account and all associated information.

How to Request Deletion

  1. In-App (Recommended): Open the Mandali app, go to your Profile tab, tap Settings, and select "Delete Account". This request is processed instantly.
  2. Via Web: If you no longer have the app installed, please click the button below to email our support team with your registered phone number/email.

What happens to my data?

Upon deletion, the following data will be permanently removed from our active servers:

  • Your personal profile information (Name, Avatar).
  • All Memories and photos you personally uploaded to any Mandali group.
  • Your membership in all Mandali groups.

Retention Period: Your data is purged immediately upon deletion. Some anonymized logs may be kept for up to 30 days for security and analytical purposes before being completely scrubbed.
