Privacy Policy

App Name: Mandali
Effective Date: April 11, 2026
Last Updated: April 11, 2026

Summary: Mandali collects only what is necessary to run the app. We do not sell your data. We do not show ads. You can request deletion of your data at any time.

1. Who We Are

Mandali ("we", "us", "our") is a group companion application that helps groups of people share memories, play Housie, and stay connected. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use the Mandali mobile application and related services.

By using Mandali, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Phone Number: Required for account creation and login via one-time password (OTP) verification.
  • Name: Required to identify you within your groups.
  • Date of Birth: Collected during profile setup to personalise your experience.
  • Profile Photo: Optional. You may choose to upload a photo to represent yourself in groups.
  • Group Photos: Photos you upload as part of the Memories feature are stored on our servers.
  • Group Information: Group names, building or society names, and group types you provide when creating groups.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Push Token: Collected to send you notifications about group activity. You can disable notifications in your device settings at any time.
  • App Usage Data: Basic information about how you use the app such as features accessed and actions performed, used to improve the product.
  • Device Information: Device type and operating system version, collected for debugging and compatibility purposes.

2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your location data.
  • We do not access your contacts list.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not collect financial payment information. All group fee tracking is informational only.
  • Mandali Glory System: Coins and symbols within the Housie feature represent "Mandali Glory" points. These points are for social standing and entertainment only; they have no cash value and cannot be redeemed for real money. No payments are processed through Mandali.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To enable you to create and join groups.
  • To store and display group photos in the Memories feature.
  • To run the Housie game feature within your groups.
  • To send you push notifications about group activity (only if you have granted permission).
  • To generate your year-end memory reel.
  • To respond to your support requests.
  • To improve and maintain the app.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your information for advertising purposes. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party.

4. How We Share Your Information

4.1 Within Your Groups

Information you share within a group — including your name, profile photo, uploaded photos, and Housie game activity — is visible to all members of that group. Please be mindful of what you share within groups.

4.2 Third-Party Service Providers

We use the following third-party services to operate Mandali. Each processes data only as necessary to provide their service:

  • Firebase (Google LLC): Used for phone number authentication and OTP verification. Privacy Policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Supabase Inc.: Used as our database to store user profiles, group data, and app records. Privacy Policy: supabase.com/privacy
  • Cloudinary Ltd.: Used to store and serve photos uploaded to the Memories feature. Privacy Policy: cloudinary.com/privacy
  • DigitalOcean LLC: Used to host our backend servers. Privacy Policy: digitalocean.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Expo (Expo Inc.): Used to deliver push notifications to your device. Privacy Policy: expo.dev/privacy

4.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law, court order, or governmental authority, or when we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, or investigate fraud.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on servers located in Bangalore, India (DigitalOcean) and Singapore (Supabase). We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS), secure token-based authentication, and access controls to protect your personal information.

While we take reasonable measures to protect your data, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

  • Account data: Retained while your account is active. Deleted within 30 days of account deletion request.
  • Group photos: Retained while you are a member of the group or until deleted by you or the group admin. Deleted within 30 days of group deletion.
  • Push tokens: Retained while the app is installed. Removed upon app uninstall or notification opt-out.
  • Authentication records: Retained by Firebase as per their data retention policy.

7. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

7.1 Access

You can view your personal information within the app at any time via your Profile screen.

7.2 Correction

You can update your name and profile photo in the app settings at any time.

7.3 Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data by emailing support@prexoratech.com or by using the Delete Account option in your Profile settings. We will process your request within 30 days.

Note: If you are an admin of a group, deleting your account will transfer admin rights to another member or dissolve the group. Photos you uploaded to group memories will be deleted. Your Housie game history will be anonymised rather than deleted to preserve group leaderboard integrity.

7.4 Notification Preferences

You can disable push notifications at any time via your device settings (iOS: Settings → Notifications → Mandali) or within the app.

7.5 Data Portability

You may request a copy of your personal data by emailing support@prexoratech.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

Mandali is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us at support@prexoratech.com and we will delete that information promptly.

Users between the ages of 13 and 18 should use the app only with the consent of a parent or guardian.

9. Cookies and Tracking

The Mandali mobile application does not use cookies. Our backend may use session tokens to maintain authentication state. These tokens are stored securely on your device and are not used for tracking or advertising purposes.

10. Third-Party Links

Mandali may allow you to share content via third-party platforms such as WhatsApp. When you use these share features, the third-party platform's own privacy policy applies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party platforms.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by sending a push notification or displaying a notice within the app. The updated policy will be effective as of the date shown at the top of this page.

Your continued use of Mandali after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact us:

  • Email: support@prexoratech.com
